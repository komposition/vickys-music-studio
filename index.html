Vicky Lee

Vicky founded her studio in Chandler, Arizona in 2002. She relocated to Las Vegas in 2008, where she serves as Musicianship Chairman of the Las Vegas Music Teachers Association.

Her teaching combines classical technique with a deep appreciation for musical expression — a belief that music helps students discover something meaningful about themselves.
